# Certifications and roadmap

## Current status

Forgestus does not hold SOC 2, FedRAMP, PCI, HIPAA or HITRUST certification or attestation. SOC 2 and FedRAMP are on the roadmap. We will not describe either as in place until it is.

## What Forge offers toward your compliance program

- **Coding rules.** HIPAA and HITRUST rule sets can be applied to every agent as coding constraints, and reviewers flag violations. Business and Enterprise.
- **Evidence.** `/evidence-pack` produces a bundle of change, review, test, deploy and cost records with an integrity manifest, mapped to HIPAA and HITRUST annexes.
- **Audit ledger.** A permanent record of agent runs on Business and Enterprise.
- **Parity evidence.** For migrations, [Parity Proof](../modernization/parity-proof.md) reports.

These support your program. They do not replace your own controls, your auditor or a Business Associate Agreement.
