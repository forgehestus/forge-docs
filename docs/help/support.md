# Support

Support is founder-led. What you get depends on your plan:

| Plan | Support |
|---|---|
| Team | Email |
| Business | Shared Slack channel |
| Enterprise | Direct access to the founder |

There is no published uptime commitment or response-time SLA.

## How to reach us

Email [support@forgestus.ai](mailto:support@forgestus.ai).

## What to send

- The link to the epic or story issue.
- The state shown in the web portal.
- Forge's last comment on the issue.
- What you already tried. See [Why is my epic stuck?](troubleshooting.md).

## Security reports

If you believe you have found a security issue in Forge, email support@forgestus.ai with "Security" in the subject. Please do not post details in a public issue.
